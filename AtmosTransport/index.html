AtmosTransport.jl experiments

Idealized comparisons of AtmosTransport.jl with GCHP, testing one transport process at a time on identical meteorology, grid and initial conditions.

Forward comparisons

  • Latest: GCHP vs AtmosTransport, process by process: null run (a GCHP 14.7.0 mass-conservation bug with transport off), full PBL mixing (perfect agreement), non-local PBL, and PBL + convection (Grell–Freitas form, convection every 240 s).
  • Boundary-layer mixing only: matching AtmosTransport to GCHP, step by step: which changes AtmosTransport needed to mix like GCHP 14.7.0, and how much each one closed the gap (cell-by-cell difference 3–5× smaller, lowest-layer fraction within 0.15–0.3 %).
  • Boundary-layer mixing only: GCHP 14.7.0 vs AtmosTransport, GEOS-IT, C90 L72, 1 Dec 2021. Surface-layer CO2 tracer, non-local PBL scheme.
  • Boundary-layer mixing only, matched air mass: the same experiment with AtmosTransport's dry air per layer computed the GEOS-Chem way; removes the 0.65% air-mass offset and halves the mixing difference.
